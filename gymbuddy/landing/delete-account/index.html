Delete Your Account

You can delete your FitnessSwipe account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1: In the app (recommended)

  1. Open FitnessSwipe and go to the Profile tab
  2. Open Settings
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

Option 2: By email

Send a deletion request from the email address linked to your account to support.fitnessswipe@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request". We will process it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, photos, bio, and preferences
  • Your matches, messages, and swipe history
  • Your location data (deleted immediately)
  • Your workout history, streaks, and challenge/duel data (including step totals)
  • Your push notification token

All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of the request. Anonymized, aggregated usage statistics (not linked to you) may be retained. Records required by law (e.g. payment records processed by Google Play or Apple) are retained by those platforms according to their policies.
